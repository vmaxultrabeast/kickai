Kickbase AI can only log in with e-mail and password. Set a password once via "Forgot password" – you can keep using Google/Apple in the Kickbase app as before.

  1. Open play.kickbase.com (log out there first if needed).
  2. Choose the login with e-mail and click „Passwort vergessen?".
  3. Enter the e-mail address of your Google/Apple account.
  4. Open the e-mail from Kickbase and choose a new password.
  5. Come back here and log in with that e-mail and password.
warningOnly use login and „Passwort vergessen?" – never register again with your e-mail. That would create a second, empty account.

For technical users: paste the kkstrauth cookie from play.kickbase.com.

Your password is sent securely only to Kickbase and is never saved. Unofficial app, not affiliated with Kickbase. Read-only unless you turn on bidding.
